# 📊 FUTURE_DS_02 – Customer Churn Analysis Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview
**FUTURE_DS_02** is a high-impact data analytics project completed during my Data Science & Analytics Internship. The primary objective was to solve the critical business problem of **Customer Attrition**. By analyzing complex telecommunications data, this project identifies exactly why customers leave and where the company is losing money.

By transforming over 7,000 rows of raw transactional data into an interactive, dark-mode executive dashboard, this project provides a data-driven roadmap to improve retention, stabilize recurring revenue, and optimize service delivery.

---

## 📈 Key Business Insights & Strategic Analysis

* **High-Risk Contractual Segments:** My analysis revealed that customers on **Month-to-Month contracts** account for the vast majority of churn. This identifies a massive opportunity to improve stability by incentivizing long-term contract migrations.
* **Service Infrastructure Vulnerability:** A critical discovery was that **Fiber Optic users** exhibit significantly higher churn rates than DSL users. This suggests a potential gap in service reliability or price-to-value perception that requires immediate quality control intervention.
* **Financial Impact & Revenue at Risk:** I successfully quantified the financial vulnerability, identifying approximately **$139.1K in Monthly Revenue** currently held by customers in the "High-Risk" category. This allows the retention team to prioritize their budget effectively.
* **The "Early-Tenure" Window:** Data trends show a 30% surge in churn within the first 6 months of a customer's lifecycle. This identifies a critical window for enhanced onboarding and proactive customer success engagement.

---

## 🧹 Data Preparation & Advanced Analysis

* **Data Engineering (ETL):** Processed raw CSV datasets using **Power Query** to ensure data integrity, handling missing values in "Total Charges" and standardizing categorical naming conventions for seamless analysis.
* **Advanced DAX Modeling:** Developed complex Calculated Measures and KPIs, including **Churn Rate %**, **Revenue at Risk**, and **Average Revenue Per User (ARPU)**, providing deeper layers of insight than standard reporting.
* **Interactive Visualization & UI/UX:** Built a dynamic, high-fidelity dashboard featuring cross-tab filtering and custom slicers. This allows stakeholders to drill down into specific demographic and service-level segments with zero latency.
